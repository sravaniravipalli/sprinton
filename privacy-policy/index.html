3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide membership and training services.
  • To plan safe and suitable workouts for you.

  • To send you updates about your membership, timings, offers, or events (you can opt out at any time).

We process your personal data with your consent, or where needed to provide the services you requested or to comply with applicable laws in India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
